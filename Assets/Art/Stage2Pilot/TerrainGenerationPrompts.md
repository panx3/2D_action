## Import preparation

Generated art is cropped and resampled with nearest-neighbor sampling to the project's 42px cells. Top and bottom bands are registered to the existing contact-line and outer-margin convention. Ramp modules are registered by their high/low contact endpoints, normalized to 42px rise, then sliced into square upper/lower cells. Concave corners are registered at their notch edges. Grate metal bands are cropped into shallow floor and fascia bands; the remaining cell area is transparent. Source alpha is retained in sampled pixels. No procedural replacement painting is used.
